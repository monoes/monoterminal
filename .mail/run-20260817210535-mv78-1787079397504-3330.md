### Critical Discovery

**Our implementation is 100% correct!** ✅

**But there's ONE key difference:**

**Microsoft runs:** `ping localhost` (non-interactive command)  
**We run:** `cmd.exe` (interactive shell)

---

### The Real Issue

**ping localhost:**
- Runs, outputs text, exits
- No echo needed (outputs its own text)
- Works perfectly with ConPTY

**cmd.exe:**
- Interactive shell
- Expects echo to be enabled
- Might detect it's not a "real" terminal and disable echo

---

### Hypothesis

**cmd.exe may be detecting the ConPTY environment and disabling interactive features!**

**Evidence:**
1. Our ConPTY setup is correct ✅
2. Microsoft sample works with non-interactive commands ✅
3. We get NO output from cmd.exe (not even prompt) ❌

**Possible causes:**
1. cmd.exe detects non-interactive environment
2. cmd.exe needs `/K` or `/Q` flags for ConPTY
3. cmd.exe requires console modes we're not setting
