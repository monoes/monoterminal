## Key Technical Decisions Locked In

**WebRTC Architecture:**
- ✅ Client-initiated WebRTC offer (standard pattern)
- ✅ Signaling over existing WebSocket connection (no separate signaling server)
- ✅ DataChannel message boundaries (no length prefix, rely on RFC 8831 framing)
- ✅ Shared sequence_number across WebSocket + DataChannel (client deduplicates)
- ✅ Dual-transport active (keep WebSocket as fallback, ~4KB overhead acceptable)

**Security:**
- ✅ PeerHandshake challenge-response protocol (cryptographic proof of Ed25519 key possession)
- ✅ 32-byte random challenge, 60s nonce window
- ✅ DTLS 1.2+ encryption (AES-GCM), equivalent to TLS 1.3
- ✅ TURN credential scoping (HMAC-bound to peer_id, 15-min TTL)

**NAT Traversal:**
- ✅ Full ICE (both client + master gather candidates)
- ✅ Trickle ICE (incremental candidate sending, 2-3s faster)
- ✅ STUN (10s) → TURN (5s) → HTTPS fallback (15s total timeout)
